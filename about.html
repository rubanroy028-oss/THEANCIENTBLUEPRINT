THE 18-YEAR VOYAGE

Rooted in Tradition, Designed for Life

"Vedic astrology isn't only for scholars in monasteries or an elite few. It belongs in everyday life. My goal is to hand you the blueprint of your own chart so you can walk through life with deep calm and clear timing."

Julie Taparia has devoted 18 years to the deep study and personal practice of Vedic Jyotish (the science of celestial light). Her study culminated in the prestigious 7-Year Parasara Jyotisa Course (PJC) taught under revered spiritual authority Pt. Sanjay Rath, one of India's most celebrated living exponents of the ancient Jaimini and Parasara traditions. Julie was formally certified in New Delhi in 2024.

Operating from New York City, Julie has counseled corporate leaders, creatives, founders, and seekers navigating crossroad decisions. Her predictions have gained widespread recognition: she appeared on national television with a Bravo TV star where she accurately predicted her upcoming marriage and precisely who she would marry, verified through Navamsha (D9) and Dasha transit alignment.

During 2022–2023, Julie served as the In-House Vedic Astrologer at Broome Street Temple NYC, conducting beloved 6-week foundational immersion courses and hands-on Vedic Astrology Bootcamps. She has also delivered masterclasses for Single to Shaadi, co-hosted Tarot & Jyotish workshops with renowned author Sasha Graham, and designed human-archetype seminars.
